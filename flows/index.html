The letters that send themselves.

Your flows are the emails that go out on their own — the moment a woman joins your list, lingers on a program, or steps away for a while. Built once, in your voice. Then they work while you sleep. Here is the whole system, mapped for the Institute.

The 80/20

Three flows do most of the quiet work.

Across automated email, a handful of sequences carry the weight. We build these first, to a finish, before we touch anything else. For a teaching-and-community practice like yours, they translate cleanly.

Flow no. 1 · highest volume

Welcome

Every woman who finds you meets this first. It turns a new name on the list into someone who feels at home — and takes her first real step.

Flow no. 2 · highest intent

She considered, then paused

The e-commerce world calls this abandoned cart. For you it is the woman who began joining the community or a program and stopped at the threshold. Warmest audience you have.

Flow no. 3 · the curious

She looked, quietly

Browse abandonment. She read the page, watched most of the video, felt the pull — and clicked away. This catches her in the window while the door is still warm.

The edge

The flows most practices skip

These are where the Sales Engine pulls ahead. Each maps a store flow to a moment that already exists in your world.

Doors are open
the waitlist flow · highest intent of all
Back-in-stock, for you. When a cohort reopens or a waitlist clears, the women waiting are the warmest audience you will ever email.
Why it matters: in stores this is the single highest-return flow. Yours is the enrollment-reopens note to a list of women who already raised their hand.
Renewal & re-enrollment
the replenishment flow · timed to your cycle
Replenishment, for you. Membership renewals and the return of a seasonal program, sent right as the last one completes its arc.
Why it matters: timed to the rhythm she's already in, renewal feels like the next breath, not a bill.
A way in
the price-drop flow · access, not discount
Price-drop, for you. Not marking the work down — a genuine access moment: founding-member pricing, a scholarship seat, a seasonal on-ramp.
Why it matters: it lets a woman who couldn't say yes at full price still come home, without cheapening the work.
Bring a woman with you
the referral flow · on-brand by nature
Post-purchase referral, for you. The work is communal — "every act of homecoming ripples to every woman near you." Referral isn't bolted on here. It's the teaching.
Why it matters: your most-aligned growth. A woman inviting a woman is the whole point, made into a flow.

Email 2 · sent Day 1

In case you wondered if it's for you

You came close the other day. You read, you felt something, and then you stepped back. I want you to know that stepping back is its own kind of honesty, and you are welcome here whenever you return.

If a quiet voice told you that you might be too much for this — too intense, too hungry, too alive — that is exactly the voice we work with. And if it told you the opposite, that you are not far enough along, not ready, not one of those women — that voice is welcome too. Both of them learned, somewhere, that it was safer to stay small.

This is not a room you have to arrive polished for. It is a room where women practice coming home to the parts of themselves they folded away. You do not need to be sure. You only need to be curious.

Just for today, notice one moment where you made yourself smaller than you are. You don't have to change it. Only see it.

And when you're ready, the door is here ›
